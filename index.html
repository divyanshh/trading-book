03 / Range of outcomes

Highlight the high. Keep the drawdown beside it.

The strongest month and the maximum recorded monthly drawdown use the same ₹20 lakh denominator. Neither is hidden inside an aggregate rupee figure.

Highest recorded month · Sep+15.3%

+₹306,757 realised on the ₹20 lakh capital base, with 23 closed trades and 56.5% winners.

Maximum recorded monthly drawdown · May−5.4%

Approximately 5% of the same capital base: −₹108,593 over 61 closed trades, with 21.3% winners.

Monthly drawdown definition−5.4% is the largest realised calendar-month loss divided by ₹20 lakh. It is not an intramonth mark-to-market peak-to-trough calculation.
Low hit rate by designOnly 32.1% of closed trades won in this window. The system depends on the average winner materially exceeding the average loss.
Short live windowSix months is one market regime. TWRR, benchmark comparison and independent attribution still remain before an investable performance presentation.
