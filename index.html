01 / Personal account record

Every fill, every trading day.

The chart follows open quantity after each broker fill, marks it to each day’s close, and transfers profit from unrealised to realised as positions are sold. It uses the full Zerodha equity account rather than the narrower strategy-only subset.

Return basisThe portfolio line is daily total P&L—realised plus unrealised—divided by a fixed ₹20,00,000 capital base. Nifty 50 uses price return from the same 1 April starting date. This avoids a false jump when an open gain becomes a booked gain.

02 / Evidence hierarchy

What is proven—and what is not.

Trust comes from preserving the boundary between broker evidence, internal reconstruction and simulation. Each answers a different question.

Broker verified

Realised account P&L

The Zerodha page independently confirms the displayed account P&L. It does not prove capital employed, percentage return, drawdown or strategy attribution.

Internally reconciled

2,373 broker fills

The fill replay reconciles to dated Zerodha P&L exports, including the final ₹477,570.33 realised total. Historical daily marks between account snapshots remain reconstructed.

Research only

Backtests and scenarios

Simulated results are labelled separately and include their capital, cost and rule assumptions. They are evidence about a model, not realised investor returns.

03 / Range of outcomes

Open profit is not booked profit.

The chart keeps unrealised and realised profit separate in every tooltip, while the comparison line uses their sum so selling a position cannot manufacture a return jump.

Realised headline₹4,77,570.33 is gross realised P&L in the Zerodha export. Broker and statutory charges shown in that export total ₹47,536.00.
Peak definition₹5.50L is peak unrealised profit, not the account’s total profit on that date. Total P&L also includes gains and losses already realised.
Reconstruction boundaryExact broker snapshots anchor the curve. Values between snapshot dates replay fills and daily closes, so they are estimates until daily broker statements are archived.
