There is a mechanical explanation for the empty books and it opens on Tuesday. Jio Platforms takes ₹1,065–1,119 a share for roughly ₹30,000 Cr on 21–23 October — comfortably the largest issue this market has seen. An institution that intends to bid for it does not tie up funds in a ₹2,534 Cr gearbox manufacturer the week before. That fits Bonfiglioli’s QIB at 0.08× far better than anything in its accounts does, and it means a weak book this week is weaker evidence about the company than it would usually be. It does not rescue the Tonbo call, and this page is not going to pretend otherwise. The same cash shortage should have emptied Tonbo’s book too and did not: QIB 3.92×, NII 5.96×, retail 3.2×, premium unmoved at 71.4% for a company whose revenue fell 22.7%, whose profit fell 30%, which lost ₹17.74 Cr in Q1 FY27 and which has run negative operating cash flow for two years. Institutions are choosing to be there. The avoid call was made on 13 October at 61% and is recorded again here at 71.4%, unchanged and so far losing. Two live scorecards now run on this page — Tonbo, where it says avoid and the market says 71.4%, and Bonfiglioli, where it says the accounts are the best on the board and the book says 0.48×. Both print within a fortnight.
