Pre-session read

Friday was the best index day of the week and it made the divergence worse, not better. The Nifty closed +1.12% at 25,800.60 — a fourth straight weekly gain, with Bank Nifty at a record and eleven of seventeen indices higher. Breadth fell from 60.25% to 53.59% while its prior-five mean fell from 68.29% to 65.13%. The gap widened from 8.04 points to 11.54, which is wider than it was on Wednesday, before Thursday’s repair. Entries stay blocked, on breadth, for a sixth consecutive session, and none of the thirty-seven actionable SWING names may be placed. (The glance bar above counts 74 across all six scanners; 37 is the SWING list alone.)

Nothing here is an instruction and nothing here was placed. Closes are Friday 20 November’s. All six scanners were re-run by hand against them in the small hours of Saturday — SWING 01:38, WEEKLY 01:49, ETF 02:14, ETF_WEEKLY 02:15, STRIKE 02:16, REVERSAL 02:27 IST — so the page carries Friday’s prices, not Saturday’s date.

Read the sign, not the size. A 1.12% index day that takes breadth down six and a half points is not a rally the market joined; it is money moving into fewer names. Bank led at +2.49%, Financial Services +2.02%, Realty +1.71% — and Media fell 1.68% while the India VIX rose 7.51% to 17.32 on a day the index closed at its highest of the month. Fear and the index rose together, which is the shape of a narrow tape, and it is the same shape MarketSmith described in Friday’s evening mail.

Why a +1.12% day cost six points of breadth

Each index’s move over the last five sessions, to Friday’s close. The large-cap benchmarks are up; everything we actually buy from is flat or down.

The spread is the whole story and it has widened again. Over the week the Nifty 50 is +2.61% while Smallcap 100 is −1.08%, Midcap 150 +0.31% and Next 50 +0.46%. PSU Bank +6.72% and Bank +5.90% carried it; Metal −2.98% and IT −1.91% paid for it. Over twenty sessions the same split holds — Nifty +14.16%, Smallcap +9.61%, IT −2.10%. A gate that reads participation rather than price is doing exactly what it was built to do here: the index is making highs out of five sectors, and the half of the market our setups live in has gone sideways for a fortnight.
