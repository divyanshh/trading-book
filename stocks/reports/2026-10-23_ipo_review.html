The Tonbo call was not unlucky. It answered the wrong question, and this page can prove it answered the right one eight days earlier. On 15 October it wrote of HD Fire: 40.7 times for 6.4% growth, entirely a promoter exit — and said apply one lot and sell on listing anyway, because the book and the premium said it would list well. That was right, +23.62%. On Tonbo it had the same two signals pointing the same way, harder — a 135.90× book, QIB 293.56×, a premium above 70% for five sessions — and overrode them with the accounts. Same evidence structure, opposite conclusion, and the only difference was that Tonbo’s accounts were worse. For a listing-day trade the accounts are not the evidence. “Is this a good business at this price” and “will this open above ₹700 on Friday” are different questions, and a 517-day working-capital cycle answers the first and says almost nothing about the second. The accounts earn their place on a HOLD, where this page never proposed to be. The GMP model had a good day and that is worth recording against Wednesday’s bad one. It implied 64% and Tonbo listed at 66% — understated by two points, where HD Fire was overstated by 7.8. Two misses in opposite directions in one week is noise around the published 2.6-point haircut, not a bias in it, and the haircut stays.

The call was avoid and it was wrong.</strong> It was made on 13 October at a 61% premium and restated on the 16th, 19th, 21st and 22nd at 71.4%, 73.7%, 74.3% and again &mdash; every time with the book getting larger and the premium holding. <strong>The accounts quoted in support of it were all true and none of them mattered.</strong> Revenue did fall 22.7%. All of that describes a business. None of it describes what a stock does on its first morning when 135.90&times; of money wants it. <strong>The footnote sets out the actual error</strong>, which is that this page applied one standard to HD Fire on 15 October and the opposite to Tonbo with the same signals in front of it.

<strong>0.34&times; on its final day with institutions at 0.06&times;.</strong> The premium has fallen 14.4% to 2.2% in seven sessions, which after the haircut this page applies is nothing at all. <strong>And the Tonbo lesson cuts the other way here, which is worth being explicit about.</strong> The argument that a weak book is not a verdict because institutions bid last is real &mdash; Bonfiglioli went 0.16&times; to 120.78&times; on its last session. So this book may yet fill. It does not change the call, because the reason to apply was never the book: it was the expected gain, and at 2.2% there is none to have even if every institution in the country arrives this afternoon.
