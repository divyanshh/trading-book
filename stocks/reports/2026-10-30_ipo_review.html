The condition was set on the 28th so it could not be rewritten on the morning it was tested, and it is not being rewritten now: QIB above 3× by Friday evening with the premium at or above 10% makes it one retail lot; anything less and it is skipped. On day-one numbers it is provisionally met — QIB 6.55× on the Kotak Neo 20:15 snapshot and GMP ₹9, 11.0% — but those are mid-window figures from a tracker, not the exchange, and another tracker has QIB at 2.82×. So the decision is tonight's, on tonight's book, and bids stay open until Monday 2 November. Odds, said as odds: about 3 in 4 that it lists above ₹82, which is a judgement from an ~8.4% haircut-adjusted premium and not a measured rate. The expected gain on one lot is roughly ₹1,250, and the spread around it is wider than the haircut. Why retail and not sHNI: NII is barely subscribed, so an sHNI bid of about ₹2.09 L would very likely be allotted in full — about fourteen times the exposure to a 2.4%-margin, heavily geared steel processor for the same ~8% expectation. That is size this book should not take on a grey-market quote.

Yesterday this was refused with one honest objection against the refusal: its premium was rising, the BLS shape in miniature. That objection has gone. InvestorGain's own history runs ₹7, ₹7, ₹8, ₹10, ₹15 on the 28th, then ₹12.5 and ₹12.5 — a peak and a fade into the closing day, the pattern SFC, EAAA and Paras all showed on this board this month. The day-2 book is between 0.70× and 1.16× depending on which tracker and which hour; every one of them has QIB under 1×. Those are mid-window numbers and the last day is when institutions usually bid, so the final book may well look better — but the call cannot wait for it, because the issue closes at 17:00. Odds, said as odds: about 2 in 3 that it lists above ₹162, with an expected gain near 5% that the measured spread cannot tell from zero. The business is genuinely growing — profit more than doubled in FY26 — and 16× is not dear against listed jewellers at 9.5–27.8×. This is a listing call, not a verdict on the company.
