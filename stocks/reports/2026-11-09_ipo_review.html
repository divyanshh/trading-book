The haircut has now been short four listings out of five, and that is no longer two listings and a shrug. The rule subtracts 2.6 points from GMP%, the overstatement backtest_gmp measured across 295 listings (r = 0.87). Against the five mainboard debuts of 2–5 November it under-called four and nailed one: BLS Polymers short by 21–26 points, SFC Environmental short by 5.7, Bombay Coated short by 3.8 (GMP 9.8% → expected ~7.2%, listed +10.98%), M.K.Sons short by 1.4 (GMP 6.8% → expected ~4.2%, listed +5.56%), and EAAA exact (GMP 3.2% → expected ~0.6%, listed +0.63%). Four misses in one direction across a single fortnight is a regime the calibration does not describe. It does not retire a 295-listing measurement on five prints — but any premium quoted on this board for the next issue should be read as a floor rather than a ceiling, and backtest_gmp is owed a re-run before the next call. The listing gain is not the trade. M.K.Sons listed +5.56% and now trades at ₹158.08, 2.42% BELOW its ₹162 issue price: the entire debut pop is gone and then some. SFC went the other way, +7.61% on debut and +25.20% now. Same fortnight, same board, opposite outcomes after day one — which is the whole argument for the horizon field carrying a condition rather than a date. GMP publishers still do not agree. niftytrader and investorgain print different final premiums on all five names, and on BLS (₹44 vs ₹69) and EAAA (₹11 vs ₹54) the gap is large enough to change the call. Direction agreed in all five cases; the level is soft and should be quoted as a range. Freshness, stated plainly: ipoji, chittorgarh, Zerodha and niftytrader carry no last-updated stamp; only investorgain stamps per row, newest 9 Nov 08:02, and every mainboard row there is last-stamped 5 Nov. The NSE upcoming-issues page timed out at 60s and BSE was not reached, so a band announced in the last few hours would not necessarily show here. This is what the aggregators and a live broker book say now, not an exchange confirmation.
