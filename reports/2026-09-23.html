How to read “apply as”: retail = one ₹15k lot, allotted by lottery when oversubscribed; sHNI = ₹2–10L, one-third of the 15% NII quota, lottery of one ₹2L lot when oversubscribed; bHNI = above ₹10L, proportionate. GMP is the grey-market premium on the morning of writing (IPO Watch 07:50 / dyno board) — unregulated, but the best single predictor we have measured; it is re-read every morning an issue stays open.
