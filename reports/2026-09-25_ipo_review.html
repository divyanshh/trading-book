By the day-2 close QIB was <b>1.82x</b> — institutions bid late, which is the ordinary shape of a book and not a surprise. The stated condition was <em>QIB under 1x at Friday's close with retail above 5x</em>. QIB is already above 1x with a day still to run, so the condition has not fired and will not unless bids are withdrawn. <b>The call lands in the same place for a different reason.</b> The original note said hold a tranche only if QIB clears 5x; at 1.82x it does not, so the whole allotment is sold at the open. At 27x on the HNI book the allotment will be a token in any case. What this costs if I am wrong: the upside beyond the first print on a small position.
