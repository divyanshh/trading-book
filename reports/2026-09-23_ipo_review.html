IPO review — apply, skip, avoid

Read at 11:45 and deepened at 12:45 IST on 23 September across Trendlyne (detail pages, brokerage-report list), Chittorgarh, the IPO Watch GMP board, Value Research, anchor circulars and independent reviews. The lens is our own backtest_gmp: over 295 listings GMP predicts the listing gain (r = 0.87) and overstates it by about 2.6 pp; a zero GMP has averaged −6.6% and listed positive 29% of the time. How to read “apply as”: retail = one ₹15k lot, allotted by lottery when oversubscribed; sHNI = ₹2–10L, one-third of the 15% NII quota, lottery of one ₹2L lot when oversubscribed; bHNI = above ₹10L, proportionate. Odds scale with the size of the category’s book — a ₹1,000 Cr issue gives far better retail odds than a ₹150 Cr one at the same oversubscription. Hand-written; none of this touches the ₹60L. Applied 23 Sep: Adroit Industries, HNI ₹2L+.
