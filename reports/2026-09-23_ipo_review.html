Brokerage “Subscribe” notes (Ventura in particular) cover nearly every issue and are not a signal. Watch before the books close: Adroit’s QIB line on Friday (zero on day 1 is normal; QIB under 1x with retail at 3x is the weak-listing pattern), Moneyview’s anchor list tonight, and whether Varmora reaches 1x at all.

Adroit Industries — APPLY. Applied 23 Sep, HNI category (₹2L+), per the owner. Sixty-year driveline maker (propeller shafts), Dewas MP, >90% exports to 32 countries. FY24→FY26 revenue 125→143 Cr, PAT 14.5→26.2 Cr (+44%), EBITDA margin 23.7→27.7% against 11–16% for GNA Axles / Talbros / Hindustan Hardy; RoE 22.5%, D/E 0.41; 88% fresh, into Dewas and Pithampur capex. Risks: top-10 customers 67% of revenue, top-10 suppliers 92% of purchases, 237-day working capital, borrowings 8→52 Cr in a year. Ventura Subscribe; Chanakya selective apply. Retail already 1.7x on day 1 with QIB at zero — the day-3 QIB book decides the listing. Small issue, highest-quality numbers on the board; retail allotment will be a lottery, HNI (₹2L+) likelier.

Moneyview — APPLY for the listing; a hold is a regulatory bet. Digital personal-loan platform: 140M registered users, 48 lending partners, managed AUM ₹21,380 Cr. FY26 revenue +43% to 3,404 Cr but PAT +1% to 243 Cr — margin compression is the number to understand. June-26 quarter PAT 174 Cr annualises to ~700 Cr (hence the 8.6x); on FY26 it is 21.5x, P/B 2.6. Expenses fell 56%→35% of income FY24→FY26. Risks: the default-loss-guarantee model is exactly where RBI has been tightening; unsecured book; top-10 partners 37% of revenue; no NPA or credit-cost disclosure in the summaries; 31% OFS; promoters 20% post-issue. Highest GMP on the board and fintech scarcity — apply; decide on holding after two quarters of credit costs are public. Check tonight's anchor list.

Orient Cables — APPLY, a fair hold candidate. #4 in networking cables (22.9% share) — broadband, telecom, data centres. FY26 revenue +42% to 1,182 Cr, PAT flat at 54 Cr (copper pass-through compressed margin 6.4→4.6%); June-26 quarter recovered to PAT 33 Cr and EBITDA 11.2%. RoE 25.8%, D/E 0.95, promoters 100→82%. P/E 23.6x vs Lumino 12.5x / Laser Power 16.2x. IIFL + JM. Electrical/power is IMPROVING on our RRG (rank 29). 42% OFS is the caveat.

A-One Steels — apply small, listing only. Southern integrated steel, 1.73 MTPA, green power under 15–25-year PPAs. FY26 revenue 4,202 Cr (+18%), PAT 7.7→127 Cr as EBITDA margin went 4.9→7.3%; RoE 14.7%, D/E 1.17; ₹250 Cr of proceeds retire debt. P/E 24.6x vs recent steel listings at 17–21x. The PAT jump is one year old and operating cash flow fell ~40% to 63 Cr — profit quality unproven; it will trade on steel prices, not this IPO.

ArMee Infotech — SKIP. IT infrastructure and managed services for government/PSU clients, Ahmedabad, plus a renewable sideline. FY26 revenue 1,410 Cr (+7%), PAT 45 Cr — below FY24's 50 Cr; margin 3.3%; RoCE 57→24% and RoE 71→29% in two years; borrowings 48→174 Cr; contingent liabilities 129 Cr; rising receivables. 26x for a 3%-margin government contractor. Its merchant bankers' last nine IPOs: seven listed below issue. The 12% GMP is the only argument.

German Green Steel & Power — SKIP for listing, watch after. TMT/billets/sponge iron, Kutch. FY26 revenue 1,685 Cr (+11%), PAT 80 Cr (+33%), EBITDA 9.9%, RoE 18.9%, D/E 0.79. P/E 13x and P/B 0.67 — the cheapest thing here — 95% fresh, ₹226 Cr into capacity plus a hybrid wind/solar plant. A regional commodity steelmaker with an 8% GMP: no listing edge, but a value name to revisit once listed if steel holds.

Swastika Infra — SKIP for listing, revisit. Power T&D EPC, Rajasthan-heavy. Growth is real — revenue 211→506 Cr, PAT 14→41 Cr in two years, RoNW 35% — and 15x is not expensive; order book 917 Cr (~1.8x revenue). But contingent liabilities 273 Cr against a ₹631 Cr market cap, rising receivables, a 4% GMP. Study after listing if the order book converts.

Runwal Enterprises — SKIP. Mumbai developer, #3 by launches and sales, 100% fresh ₹500 Cr. FY26 income 1,851 Cr / PAT 186 Cr — but FY24 was 2,437 / 94 and FY25 1,051 / 56: recognition is lumpy and the +234% PAT year is not a run-rate. D/E 3.29, borrowings 2,909 Cr, P/B 5.0, P/E 24x; only ₹100 Cr of proceeds to debt. GMP 6%. ICICI Sec + Jefferies.

Varmora Granito — AVOID. Morbi tiles. Revenue flat three years (1,473→1,563 Cr), PAT 55 Cr, RoE 6.8%, P/E 60.7x vs Kajaria ~40x with double the returns; 55% OFS (Katsura exiting); every fresh rupee to debt repayment. Brokerages split — Anand Rathi / Axis / Antique / Ventura Subscribe, Canara Avoid. The book has voted: 0.15x on day 2, QIB 0.00x.

Elevate Campuses — AVOID at the IPO. Student housing (78,542 beds) plus K-12 schools. The business is good (Value Research: "good business, complicated issue"); the issue is not. FY26 PAT 174 Cr includes a ₹105 Cr one-time gain — normalised P/E ~68x (Value Research: 89x on 69 Cr). D/E 4.98, borrowings 1,207→4,121 Cr in one year. Of the ₹2,100 Cr fresh issue, ₹1,100 Cr goes to the sponsor's Singapore entities to buy 16 private schools; ₹750 Cr to debt. Anchors ₹945 Cr (JM / IIFL / Morgan Stanley). GMP 1.4% → listing flat to negative. Interesting below ~₹270 with verified debt reduction, not at 362.

AceVector (Snapdeal / Unicommerce / Stellaro) — AVOID. Loss-making: FY26 net loss 61 Cr on revenue 510 Cr (+29%); SoftBank and Nexus selling in the OFS; ₹1,750 Cr valuation. GMP zero — our base rate for a zero-GMP listing is −6.6%, positive 29% of the time.
