What the two results actually teach, separately from whether they felt good. One: the GMP model overstated by three times its historical margin. This page applies a 2.6-point haircut to every grey-market premium, measured over 295 listings. HD Fire quoted 31.4% and listed at 23.62% — an overstatement of 7.8 points. The call was still right and the direction was still right, but anyone sizing on “~+29%” got three quarters of it. One listing is not a reason to change the haircut; it is a reason to write the miss down and watch whether it repeats, because a model that is wrong in the same direction twice is a model with a bias rather than noise. Two: a weak book on day two says almost nothing. Bonfiglioli went 0.16× to 120.78× on QIB in one session, which is the second time this month the same pattern printed — HD Fire went 1.49× to 256.65×. Two for two is not proof, but it is now the base case here, and a page that reads Friday’s institutional number as a verdict will keep being wrong in the same direction. And the correction. The 19 October entry said retail allotment was “near-certain” because retail sat at 1.16×. It closed at 5.89×. That is roughly one lot in six, not a near-certainty, and the error was treating a day-two retail figure as settled while arguing in the very same entry that a day-two QIB figure was not. The argument was right; it simply was not applied to both columns.
