Why the condition is not being rewritten. Bidding opens at 10:00 and runs to 17:00, so the issue is open and the condition is not. It was set on 28 October, read at Friday’s close, and it said skip. Re-opening it today, after seeing the answer, would be writing a new condition to get a different one — which is the single thing a pre-set condition exists to prevent. If it is to be re-opened, that is his call made knowingly, not a drift in the paperwork. The trackers still disagree, and by more than the decision needs. At the 30 October close IPOji reads 3.98× overall with QIB 6.89×, NII 1.89× and retail 3.17×; Kotak Neo, stamped 20:15 the same evening, reads 3.74× with QIB 6.56×, NII 1.75× and retail 2.95×. Neither changes the GMP leg, which is the one that failed. The haircut, applied: GMP% minus 2.6 points, and the spread around it is wider than the haircut — −3 to +7.5 points on the last three graded listings. A 9.76% premium is therefore about 7.2% expected, inside the spread. A date to hold on to: the lesson taken from BLS Polymers on 30 October is that a subscription figure is only usable with the hour it was read, and a book quoted before the close is a floor, not a measurement. Every multiple on this page carries its timestamp for that reason.

The condition is the whole decision, so it is worth stating plainly. On 28 October the test was written as: QIB above 3 times by Friday, and GMP at or above 10 percent. Read at Friday's 17:05 close, QIB was 6.89 times on both trackers that print a category split, so that leg passed and is not arguable. GMP was 7 rupees, which on an 82 rupee cap is 8.5 percent, and both trackers agreed on it. The 10 percent line is 8.20 rupees. The leg failed. The premium has moved since without clearing the line: 9 rupees through most of last week, 8.5 at 15:25 on Friday, 7 at 17:05, and 8 this morning at 06:45 on IPOji, which is 9.76 percent. InvestorGain still displays 9 rupees, but the page carries a last-updated stamp of 29 October 23:40, so that figure is three days stale and is not used here. After the 2.6 point haircut, 9.76 percent is about 7.2 percent expected, which sits inside the spread the haircut itself carries, so the premium is not distinguishable from a flat listing. The book is day two and not final. Bidding opens at 10:00 and runs to 17:00 today, so no day-3 figure exists yet. At the 30 October close IPOji has 3.98 times overall, QIB 6.89, NII 1.89, retail 3.17. Kotak Neo, stamped 20:15 the same evening, has 3.74 overall, QIB 6.56, NII 1.75, retail 2.95. Retail at about 3 times means roughly one application in three gets a lot, which is better odds than the issue deserves on its merits. On the merits nothing has improved. 125 crore of the 183 crore raised repays debt rather than building anything. Profit grew 6.5 percent on revenue up 20 percent, a net margin of 2.4 percent, and debt to equity reads between 2.00 and 2.78 depending on the source. The call is SKIP and nothing has been applied for.
