Thursday opens with one gate open and the other at its worst reading of the move. Read the open gate carefully. A gate that turns green on a sixteen-day-old event while the breadth it is meant to confirm collapses is not a buy signal, it is a lag. The two readings are measuring different things: the follow-through looks backwards at one day in early November, breadth looks at what 877 names did on Wednesday. On this evidence the breadth gate is the one carrying information.
