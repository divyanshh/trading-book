Is Bonfiglioli’s book a verdict or just early? This board already answered that once. HD Fire sat at QIB 1.49× with one session to run and this page called it the issue’s only soft spot. It closed at 256.65×. Institutions bid on the last day, so 0.16× on a Friday is not the number to judge an issue by — tonight’s is. What would make it a verdict is the same figure at the close. Two live calls settle this week and both are recorded before the print. Tonbo: avoid, made 13 October at a 61% premium, restated at 71.4% and now at 73.7% against a 7.78× book. It is losing and it is not being revised, because a call quietly rewritten once the market disagrees is not a record. Bonfiglioli: the best accounts on the board, carrying a third of Tonbo’s premium and a book a fiftieth the size. One of those two readings of the same week is wrong and the listings will say which. The one thing that has gone exactly as written is HD Fire, where the day-2 QIB worry was called wrong in public on the 16th and the final book proved it.
