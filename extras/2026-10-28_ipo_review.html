How the Jio number went wrong, since the mechanism matters more than the apology. The figures were taken from the subscription tracker during the evening of the 23rd and written down as the close. They were a 6-ish PM snapshot: institutions bid in the last hour, which is the exact behaviour this page has argued twice this month — Bonfiglioli went 0.16× to 120.78× on its final day — and then failed to allow for in its own record. The direction of the error is the uncomfortable part. After Tonbo, this page wrote that for a listing-day trade the book is the evidence and the accounts are not. It then under-recorded that evidence by a factor of five on QIB. The call — apply, sell into the listing — is unchanged and was right; the number under it was wrong, and a right call on a wrong number is luck. The shareholders’ quota, 7.60×, is worth recording too. It existed, it was contested, and it is the reason a single Reliance share was worth holding into the 23rd. That inference was correct.
