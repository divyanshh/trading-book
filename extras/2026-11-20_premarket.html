Pre-session read

Thursday was a strong index day and it did almost nothing for the gate. The Nifty gapped down to 25,104.80, turned, and closed at the day’s high — +1.14% to 25,514.30, with Bank Nifty at a record and Auto +4.40%. Breadth moved from 59.52% to 59.57% while its prior-five mean rose to 70.39%. Five hundredths of a point on a one-and-a-fifth percent index day. Entries remain blocked, on breadth, and none of tonight’s thirty actionable names may be placed.

Nothing here is an instruction and nothing here was placed. Closes are Thursday 19 November’s. All six scanners were re-run by hand against them once the bhavcopy landed — SWING run 491, STRIKE 492, WEEKLY 493, ETF 494, ETF_WEEKLY 495, REVERSAL 498.

What is news is the shape of the day: the money went into PSU banks, FMCG, banks and autos — the heavyweights — and the broad market did not come with it.
