The BLS call is made on structure, not on the premium, and the distinction matters after this month. What is checkable: ₹255 Cr entirely fresh, 1.70 Cr shares, a stated use of proceeds that puts ₹77.56 Cr into plant capacity and ₹89.50 Cr into working capital, and roughly 25 times FY26 diluted earnings of ₹5.99 at the top of the band. Those are facts from the terms. What is not checkable, and is said rather than hidden: no revenue, profit or margin figures could be read this morning, and there is no subscription book for a second day. So this is a call on how the issue is built and what the money does, with two of the three columns missing. It is also late. On the 26th this page wrote that the prospectus would be read overnight and the call would go up before the book opened. It did not. The issue runs to Thursday so the application is still live, but the commitment was missed and saying so is cheaper than pretending the timing was always Tuesday.
