Tonbo closed at 135.90× with QIB 293.56× and listed +66% against this page’s avoid, which is why the book outranks the accounts for a listing-day trade. Quoting a GMP and calling it analysis would be the same mistake with better manners. The calls come when the books open. BLS at 25.3% is the one worth reading the prospectus for before tomorrow.
