<strong>The call this page owed before the open, and it is apply.</strong> Three things carry it. It is <em>entirely</em> fresh capital &mdash; alone on this month&rsquo;s board in that, where Bonfiglioli, Tonbo and HD Fire were all full or partial exits and the money left the company. The premium has risen every single session for a week while every other issue on the board except Tonbo was marked down, which is the one direction that matters going into an open. And the retail quota of a &#8377;30,213 Cr issue is enormous in absolute terms, so allotment is a better bet here than on a small hot issue &mdash; the inverse of HD Fire&rsquo;s one-in-thirty. <strong>The caveat is what the money does, and it is not small.</strong> &#8377;27,500 Cr of the &#8377;30,213 Cr repays debt. That is real and it is good for the company &mdash; interest cost comes down, the balance sheet improves &mdash; but anyone buying this as a growth issue should know that nine rupees in ten are retiring borrowings rather than building anything. <strong>And size cuts the upside.</strong> An issue this large is hard to squeeze, so the listing is likely to be more orderly than a 25% premium implies; ~+22% after the overstatement this page applies is the expectation, not a floor. One lot, retail, sold into the listing on 28 October. <strong>No hold case is being made</strong> &mdash; that needs the prospectus financials read properly, and this is a call about an application closing Friday.

<strong>The premium halved on the morning it opened</strong>, 13.7% to 7.2%, which after the 2.6-point overstatement leaves about five per cent. A grey market that marks an issue down <em>as it opens</em> is saying something about the book it expects. <strong>Seventy-two per cent of the raise is a shareholder leaving.</strong> &#8377;1,300 Cr of &#8377;1,800 Cr goes to the selling holder and &#8377;500 Cr to the company. Set against the issue opening the same morning &mdash; wholly fresh capital, premium rising &mdash; this is the worse of the two in every respect that can be checked before a book exists. <strong>Skip.</strong> The prospectus work this page promised is moot: the market answered first and the structure was never attractive.
