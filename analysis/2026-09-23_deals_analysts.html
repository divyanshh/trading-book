Deals

The tab is market makers, not mutual funds

The intuition — "if a fund is accumulating, that is a tell" — is sound. The data does not carry it. A bulk or block deal is only reported when somebody crosses a threshold in a single trade, and on our universe the names doing that are almost entirely proprietary and high-frequency desks, most of them printing a BUY and a SELL of identical size on the same day. That is market-making, not a view.

Why this ends the question

Long-money institutions appear in the 90 days before an entry on 4 of 132 trades, and in the 30 days before on 0. A predictor absent on 97% of the book cannot be tested, however well it does on the remainder. Real fund accumulation happens below the bulk-deal threshold, over weeks — it shows up in shareholding patterns, which is a quarterly disclosure, not a daily one.

What this means

"Covered by any analyst" reads +3.63pt, p=0.039 across the whole window. Split it: the first half is −0.41pt (p=0.85) and the second is +7.60pt (p=0.004). A signal that is absent in one half and enormous in the other is a description of a period, not of a rule. Removing the three largest winners takes the whole-window gap from +3.63pt to +1.59pt, which says the same thing a different way.

It is also not simply a size proxy: the gap persists across all three liquidity terciles (+5.2, +4.0, +3.5 points). That makes it more interesting and no more tradeable.

What to do

The data is worth keeping, as description

Do not gate on either

Neither deals nor ratings earn a gate. The standing rule applies: rank and record first, earn the gate later.

Wire the rating history into the report

The endpoint returns every brokerage call with its date, the price at recommendation and the target — richer and better dated than the coverage source the report uses now. Useful as context beside a setup, not as a filter.

Leave deals alone

On this universe the tab is an HFT print log. Revisit only if the book moves into names large enough for funds to cross in single trades.

How to read this

Method, and its limits

Every signal is measured strictly before the entry date of a trade the book actually took, then compared against the realised return of that trade. Significance is a sign-flip permutation test over 5,000 shuffles; the split is the calendar median of the 132 entries. This is a test on fills, not on the candidate population — a fills-only test can only ask "among the trades we took, did this separate them", which is the right question here because both signals were being proposed as a filter on names already selected.
