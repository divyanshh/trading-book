RS is a percentile of the 30-day return across the measured universe on the same completed UTC day, 1 to 99 — computed here, not bought. Equity's RS rating is MarketSmith's own field and there is no such vendor for perpetuals, so the two share a scale and a meaning but are not the same number. Nothing refuses a symbol for its RS.

It is relative and only relative. On 2026-11-09 BTC rated 82 while down 9.18% over thirty days, because most of the universe fell harder. A high RS in a falling market means fell less, not rose — read it beside the move column, never instead of it.
