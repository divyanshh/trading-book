Nothing here waits for a person — these rows are orders the service will place, or has placed, subject to the position, risk and notional caps.

Placed on a trigger — `plan-automatic-tis` ranks one plan at 00:45 UTC and holds it 48 hours; `execute-automatic-tis` checks every hour, arms on a high through the trigger, and then submits an immediate-or-cancel limit. An unfilled order never rests.
